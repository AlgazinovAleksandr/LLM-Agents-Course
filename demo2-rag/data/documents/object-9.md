# Object 9 - "Dumb Gum"

# Physical Description

**Object 9**, also commonly referred to as **Dumb Gum**, is a soft, edible substance that's visually similar to chewing gum found in the Frontrooms. It has a generally rubbery consistency due to its composition of melted sand, though remains relatively solid when left untouched due to the formation process which compacts its interior.

**Dumb Gum** can be found in a variety of different colors and shapes, though mainly appears as either a light violet or warm orange color. This is largely due to its formation process; it starts out as sand deep below the dunes of [Level 201](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-201), but pressure of the sand above it causes it to slowly rise in heat. This heat melts the sand, turning it into a soft substance, which will slowly cool down and harden over time, producing instances of **Object 9**. The form that the **Dumb Gum** shapes into is dependent on which area of Level 201 it is created in. If made along the shore, waves from the ocean will cause the molten sand to rise in large sheets, which would then subsequently break into their respective rectangular pieces. If made in one of the more chilly sections of the level, the cold will cause it to harden and compact into smaller pieces which also have a much firmer exterior.

# Psychological Effects

When consumed, **Dumb Gum** doesn't appear to cause any immediate abnormal effects. It has been described as having an extremely citrusy flavor which may cause one to spit it out upon first trying it. Individuals who have consumed this have stated that this flavor is very unique and they haven't been able to recreate it any other way, despite many attempts. Some individuals have been reported to regularly intake **Object 9** and suffer its effects despite the mental strain it causes them.

Thirty minutes after consumption, recipients of the effects claim to feel a painful burning sensation within the back of their throats which gets increasingly irritating as time goes on, ceasing as soon as the next set of effects take place. Individuals will then slowly begin to lose motor skills, such as being able to stand, intake liquids, walking, talking, swimming, and, in rare cases, the ability to blink or breathe twenty minutes after the burning sensation is experienced. Five minutes following these effects, the individual affected will then faint for around an hour, waking up with no memory of what transpired whilst they were under its influence.
